# Calculation

* [Artifact Exhibition Compatibility (AEC)](artifact-exhibition-compatibility.md) - Determines how compatible an artifact is with its current showcase environment.
* [Artifact Vulnerability Score (AVS)](artifact-vulnerability-score.md) - Comprehensive score indicating how vulnerable an artifact is based on its conservation priority and environmental sensitivities.
* [Conservation Backlog Risk (CBR)](conservation-backlog-risk.md) - Quantifies the risk associated with delayed conservation treatments.
* [Conservation Budget Efficiency (CBE)](conservation-budget-efficiency.md) - Measures the efficiency of conservation budget allocation relative to artifact importance.
* [Conservation Priority Index (CPI)](conservation-priority-index.md) - Calculates the overall conservation priority for an artifact based on multiple factors.
* [Conservation Resource Allocation Efficiency (CRAE)](conservation-resource-allocation-efficiency.md) - Measures how efficiently conservation resources are allocated based on priorities and budget.
* [Display Safety Duration (DSD)](display-safety-duration.md) - Calculates the recommended maximum display duration for an artifact based on its sensitivities.
* [Environmental Compliance Index (ECI)](environmental-compliance-index.md) - Measures how well current environmental conditions meet the requirements for an artifact.
* [Environmental Risk Factor (ERF)](environmental-risk-factor.md) - Quantifies the overall environmental risk to an artifact based on its sensitivities.
* [Exhibition Rotation Priority Score (ERPS)](exhibition-rotation-priority-score.md) - Calculates priority for rotating artifacts in and out of exhibition based on multiple factors.
* [Exhibition Safety Quotient (ESQ)](exhibition-safety-quotient.md) - Comprehensive safety rating for an exhibition based on artifacts, showcases, and visitor factors.
* [Light Exposure Risk (LER)](light-exposure-risk.md) - Quantifies the risk from light exposure based on artifact sensitivity and current light levels.
* [Material Aging Projection (MAP)](material-aging-projection.md) - Projects the rate of artifact aging based on material type and environmental conditions.
* [Material Deterioration Rate (MDR)](material-deterioration-rate.md) - Estimates the rate of material deterioration based on environmental factors.
* [Security Risk Exposure (SRE)](security-risk-exposure.md) - Quantifies an artifact's exposure to security risks based on value and security measures.
* [Showcase Environmental Stability Rating (SESR)](showcase-environmental-stability-rating.md) - Measures how well a showcase maintains stable environmental conditions.
* [Showcase Protection Adequacy (SPA)](showcase-protection-adequacy.md) - Measures how well a showcase protects its artifacts based on its stability and the artifacts' requirements.
* [Total Environmental Threat Level (TETL)](total-environmental-threat-level.md) - Comprehensive measurement of all environmental threats to an artifact based on multiple risk factors.
* [Visitor Capacity Safety Factor (VCSF)](visitor-capacity-safety-factor.md) - Determines the safe visitor capacity for exhibition halls containing sensitive artifacts.
* [Visitor Impact Risk (VIR)](visitor-impact-risk.md) - Assesses the risk posed by visitor traffic to artifacts in exhibition halls.
