# Definition

Fuel cell efficiency percentages typically range from 40% to 90%. Values below 50% represent older or degraded fuel cell technology, 50-70% indicate standard efficiency modern fuel cells suitable for general applications, and values above 70% represent high-performance fuel cells with optimal conversion of chemical energy to electrical power with minimal waste heat generation.
