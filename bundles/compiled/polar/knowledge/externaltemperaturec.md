# Definition

External temperature in polar regions typically ranges from -70°C to 10°C. Temperatures below -40°C represent extreme cold requiring special equipment protection measures, -20°C to -40°C require standard cold weather protocols, while temperatures above -20°C are considered relatively mild for polar operations but still require normal cold weather precautions.
