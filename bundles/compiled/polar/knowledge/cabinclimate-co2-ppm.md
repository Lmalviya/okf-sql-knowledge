# Definition

CO2 levels in enclosed spaces like cabins are measured in parts per million (ppm). Levels below 600 ppm indicate excellent ventilation, 600-1000 ppm represent good air quality, 1000-2500 ppm indicate poor ventilation that may cause drowsiness and reduced cognitive function, while levels above 2500 ppm may cause headaches, sleepiness, and significantly impaired cognitive performance.
