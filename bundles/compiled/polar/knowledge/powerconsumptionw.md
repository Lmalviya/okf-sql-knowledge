# Definition

Power consumption measured in watts varies by equipment type. Small scientific instruments typically consume 5-50W, communication equipment 20-200W, heating systems 500-5000W, and vehicle systems 1000-10000W. Understanding consumption patterns is crucial for power budgeting and determining appropriate power source sizing in isolated polar environments.
