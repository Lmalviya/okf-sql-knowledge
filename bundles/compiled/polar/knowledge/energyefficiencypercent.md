# Definition

Energy efficiency percentage typically ranges from 10% to 99%. Values below 30% indicate inefficient systems typical of older equipment, 30-60% represent standard efficiency for conventional equipment, 60-80% indicate high-efficiency modern systems, while values above 80% represent cutting-edge technology with optimal efficiency that minimizes energy waste and operational costs.
