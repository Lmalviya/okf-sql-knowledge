# Calculation

* [Account Activity Frequency (AAF)](account-activity-frequency.md) - Measures how frequently an account engages in platform activities relative to its age.
* [Authentication Pattern Score (APS)](authentication-pattern-score.md) - Evaluates consistency of authentication behaviors.
* [Authentication Risk Score (ARS)](authentication-risk-score.md) - Assesses authentication-related risks.
* [Automated Behavior Score (ABS)](automated-behavior-score.md) - Measures degree of automation in account behavior.
* [Behavioral Anomaly Score (BAS)](behavioral-anomaly-score.md) - Quantifies unusual behavior patterns.
* [Behavioral Consistency Score (BCS)](behavioral-consistency-score.md) - Measures consistency of account behavior patterns.
* [Bot Behavior Index (BBI)](bot-behavior-index.md) - Combines multiple bot-detection metrics into a single score.
* [Content Amplification Effect (CAE)](content-amplification-effect.md) - Measures the cascade effect of content sharing.
* [Content Authenticity Score (CAS)](content-authenticity-score.md) - Aggregates multiple authenticity indicators into a single score.
* [Content Distribution Pattern Score (CDPS)](content-distribution-pattern-score.md) - Analyzes patterns in content posting and sharing.
* [Content Impact Score (CIS)](content-impact-score.md) - Measures potential impact of manipulated content.
* [Content Manipulation Score (CMS)](content-manipulation-score.md) - Evaluates content manipulation patterns.
* [Content Security Index (CSI)](content-security-index.md) - Evaluates content security considering manipulation and authenticity.
* [Coordinated Activity Score (CAS)](coordinated-activity-score.md) - Measures likelihood of coordinated behavior across accounts.
* [Coordinated Bot Risk (CBR)](coordinated-bot-risk.md) - Assesses risk from coordinated bot networks.
* [Cross-Platform Correlation Score (CPCS)](cross-platform-correlation-score.md) - Measures behavioral correlation across platforms.
* [Cross-Platform Risk Index (CPRI)](cross-platform-risk-index.md) - Evaluates risk across multiple platform types for the same account.
* [Enhanced Trust Score (ETS)](enhanced-trust-score.md) - Calculates trust score considering both profile credibility and content authenticity.
* [influence ranking by NIC](influence-ranking-by-nic.md) - A ranking system that orders accounts based on their NIC scores from highest to lowest.
* [last activity proxy time](last-activity-proxy-time.md) - An estimated timestamp of the last known activity for an account, used when direct session timestamps are unavailable or insufficient.
* [Latest Bot Likelihood Score (LBS)](latest-bot-likelihood-score.md) - The most recent bot likelihood score for an account based on security detection timestamps.
* [maximum coordination score](maximum-coordination-score.md) - The highest coordination score observed among the members of an identified cluster.
* [member account IDs](member-account-ids.md) - A collection (array) of the unique account indexes belonging to an identified cluster.
* [member count](member-count.md) - The total number of unique accounts within an identified cluster.
* [Moderation Priority Score (MPS)](moderation-priority-score.md) - Calculates priority for moderation review.
* [Multi-Account Correlation Index (MACI)](multi-account-correlation-index.md) - Measures behavioral correlation across linked accounts.
* [Network Growth Velocity (NGV)](network-growth-velocity.md) - Measures the rate of network growth considering both followers and following.
* [Network Influence Centrality (NIC)](network-influence-centrality.md) - Quantifies account's position and influence in interaction network.
* [Network Manipulation Index (NMI)](network-manipulation-index.md) - Measures the extent of network manipulation considering bot behavior and coordination.
* [Network Synchronization Index (NSI)](network-synchronization-index.md) - Quantifies synchronized activities across account clusters.
* [Network Trust Score (NTS)](network-trust-score.md) - Evaluates trustworthiness of account's network connections.
* [Profile Credibility Index (PCI)](profile-credibility-index.md) - Evaluates overall profile credibility.
* [Reputation Volatility Index (RVI)](reputation-volatility-index.md) - Quantifies stability of account reputation over time.
* [Security Risk Score (SRS)](security-risk-score.md) - Calculates overall security risk based on multiple factors.
* [Session Count (SC)](session-count.md) - Measures the total number of session records associated with an account.
* [Technical Evasion Index (TEI)](technical-evasion-index.md) - Quantifies attempts to evade detection.
* [TEI quartile](tei-quartile.md) - Categorizes accounts into four groups based on their TEI values.
* [Temporal Pattern Deviation Score (TPDS)](temporal-pattern-deviation-score.md) - Measures deviation from established temporal activity patterns.
* [Total Post Frequency (TPF)](total-post-frequency.md) - Measures the total posting frequency across all sessions for an account.
