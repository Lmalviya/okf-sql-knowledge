# Calculation

* [Audit Compliance Pressure (ACP)](audit-compliance-pressure.md) - Quantifies pressure from audit findings and compliance remediation needs.
* [Audit Finding Severity (AFS)](audit-finding-severity.md) - Quantifies the severity of audit findings based on critical findings.
* [Audit Remediation Load (ARL)](audit-remediation-load.md) - Calculates the workload required for audit remediation based on findings and compliance gaps.
* [Bandwidth Compliance Risk (BCR)](bandwidth-compliance-risk.md) - Assesses compliance risk from bandwidth-constrained data flows.
* [Bandwidth Risk Factor (BRF)](bandwidth-risk-factor.md) - Evaluates risk from bandwidth overuse in sensitive data flows.
* [Bandwidth Saturation Index (BSI)](bandwidth-saturation-index.md) - Quantifies how close a data flow is to saturating available bandwidth.
* [Compliance Cost Ratio (CCR)](compliance-cost-ratio.md) - Evaluates the cost of compliance relative to potential penalties.
* [Compliance Overhead Ratio (COR)](compliance-overhead-ratio.md) - Measures the operational burden of compliance relative to data subject request load.
* [Cross-Border Compliance Exposure (CBCE)](cross-border-compliance-exposure.md) - Quantifies compliance risk for cross-border flows based on regulatory gaps and volume.
* [Cross-Border Data Volume Risk (CDVR)](cross-border-data-volume-risk.md) - Assesses risk from large cross-border data volumes.
* [Cross-Border Risk Factor (CBRF)](cross-border-risk-factor.md) - Evaluates risk associated with cross-border data transfers.
* [Data Flow Reliability Score (DFRS)](data-flow-reliability-score.md) - Quantifies the reliability of a data flow based on success rate and retry attempts.
* [Data Flow Stability Index (DFSI)](data-flow-stability-index.md) - Quantifies the stability of data flows by balancing reliability and error recovery.
* [Data Retention Risk Score (DRRS)](data-retention-risk-score.md) - Evaluates risk from prolonged data retention relative to sensitivity.
* [Data Sensitivity Index (DSI)](data-sensitivity-index.md) - Quantifies the sensitivity of data based on volume and sensitivity level.
* [Data Subject Request Load (DSRL)](data-subject-request-load.md) - Measures the workload from data subject requests.
* [Data Subject Request Pressure (DSRP)](data-subject-request-pressure.md) - Quantifies pressure from data subject requests relative to response time.
* [Data Transfer Efficiency (DTE)](data-transfer-efficiency.md) - Measures the efficiency of data transfers based on success rate and error count.
* [Encryption Coverage Ratio (ECR)](encryption-coverage-ratio.md) - Measures the extent of encryption coverage relative to data sensitivity.
* [Incident Impact Factor (IIF)](incident-impact-factor.md) - Measures the potential impact of incidents based on risk exposure and resolution efficiency.
* [Incident Resolution Efficiency (IRE)](incident-resolution-efficiency.md) - Measures how efficiently incidents are resolved relative to SLA compliance.
* [Integrity Failure Count (IFC)](integrity-failure-count.md) - Counts the number of failed integrity checks per data profile.
* [Risk Exposure Score (RES)](risk-exposure-score.md) - Calculates the overall risk exposure by combining risk assessment and residual risk.
* [Security Control Cost Ratio (SCCR)](security-control-cost-ratio.md) - Evaluates the cost-effectiveness of security controls relative to compliance costs.
* [Security Posture Maturity (SPM)](security-posture-maturity.md) - Evaluates the maturity of security controls based on encryption and audit log retention.
* [Security Robustness Score (SRS)](security-robustness-score.md) - Measures the strength of security controls based on encryption and access controls.
* [Vendor Compliance Burden (VCB)](vendor-compliance-burden.md) - Measures the compliance burden of a vendor based on audit findings and security rating.
* [Vendor Reliability Index (VRI)](vendor-reliability-index.md) - Assesses vendor reliability based on security rating and contract status.
* [Vendor Risk Amplification (VRA)](vendor-risk-amplification.md) - Quantifies how vendor issues amplify overall risk exposure.
* [Vendor Risk Concentration (VRC)](vendor-risk-concentration.md) - Assesses the concentration of risk from a vendor’s compliance and security issues.
* [Vendor Security Cost Index (VSCI)](vendor-security-cost-index.md) - Evaluates the cost-effectiveness of vendor security relative to compliance burden.
