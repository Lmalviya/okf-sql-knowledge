# Definition

Describes atmospheric conditions with minimal turbulence, allowing for sharp, detailed observations. Typically corresponds to image stability better than 1 arcsecond and is often associated with stable temperature gradients and low wind speeds ($\text{WindSpeedMs} < 2.5$).
