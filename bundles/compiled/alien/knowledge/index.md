# Calculation

* [Artificial Intelligence Detection Probability (AIDP)](artificial-intelligence-detection-probability.md) - Calculates likelihood of artificial intelligence origin based on encoding complexity and technosignature indicators.
* [Atmospheric Observability Index (AOI)](atmospheric-observability-index.md) - Quantifies how conducive atmospheric conditions are for signal detection.
* [Bandwidth-Frequency Ratio (BFR)](bandwidth-frequency-ratio.md) - Measures the proportion of bandwidth to center frequency, helping identify signal type.
* [Bandwidth-to-Frequency Ratio (BFR)](bandwidth-to-frequency-ratio.md) - Normalized signal width relative to its central frequency.
* [CCS Approximation](ccs-approximation.md) - Simplified CCS calculation using direct signal-to-noise ratio values when full Signal-to-Noise Quality Indicator (SNQI) data is unavailable.
* [Celestial Location Significance Factor (CLSF)](celestial-location-significance-factor.md) - Calculates significance of signal source location based on astronomical targets of interest.
* [Confirmation Confidence Score (CCS)](confirmation-confidence-score.md) - Quantifies overall confidence in signal verification across multiple parameters.
* [Detection Instrument Sensitivity Factor (DISF)](detection-instrument-sensitivity-factor.md) - Calculates the effective sensitivity of the detection setup based on telescope and environmental factors.
* [Encoding Complexity Index (ECI)](encoding-complexity-index.md) - Evaluates the sophistication of potential encoding in the signal.
* [Habitable Zone Signal Relevance (HZSR)](habitable-zone-signal-relevance.md) - Assesses signal relevance based on source's position in habitable zone.
* [Information Entropy Ratio (IER)](information-entropy-ratio.md) - Compares signal entropy to expected natural background entropy.
* [Lunar Interference Factor (LIF)](lunar-interference-factor.md) - Calculates the potential interference from lunar illumination on observations.
* [Modulation Complexity Score (MCS)](modulation-complexity-score.md) - Quantifies the sophistication of signal modulation based on type and stability.
* [Observation Quality Factor (OQF)](observation-quality-factor.md) - Provides a comprehensive measure of observational conditions quality.
* [Pattern Recognition Confidence (PRC)](pattern-recognition-confidence.md) - Measures confidence in identified signal patterns based on multiple factors.
* [Research Priority Index (RPI)](research-priority-index.md) - Helps researchers prioritize signals for follow-up based on multiple factors.
* [Signal Complexity Ratio (SCR)](signal-complexity-ratio.md) - Measures the relationship between signal complexity and information density.
* [Signal Processing Efficiency Index (SPEI)](signal-processing-efficiency-index.md) - Evaluates the computational efficiency of signal processing relative to complexity.
* [Signal Stability Metric (SSM)](signal-stability-metric.md) - Quantifies overall temporal and spectral stability of a signal.
* [Signal-to-Noise Quality Indicator (SNQI)](signal-to-noise-quality-indicator.md) - Combines SNR and noise floor to provide a unified signal quality metric.
* [Technological Origin Likelihood Score (TOLS)](technological-origin-likelihood-score.md) - Combines multiple factors to estimate likelihood of technological origin.
