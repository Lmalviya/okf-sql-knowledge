# Definition

Describes electromagnetic waves where the electric field vector rotates in a circular pattern as the wave propagates. Circular polarization maintaining high purity across frequency (indicated by $\text{PolarAngleDeg}$ stability) is rare in natural sources and may indicate technological origin.
