# Business Rule

* [Complex Care Needs](complex-care-needs.md) - Identifies patients requiring intensive care coordination due to multiple risk factors.
* [Facility Attrition Risk Indicator](facility-attrition-risk-indicator.md) - Identifies facilities potentially experiencing high patient dropout, characterized by low engagement/adherence and high missed appointment rates.
* [Facility Demonstrating Strong Patient Retention](facility-demonstrating-strong-patient-retention.md) - Identifies facilities showing positive performance indicators related to high patient adherence and low missed appointment rates.
* [Facility Performance Quadrant (FPQ)](facility-performance-quadrant.md) - Categorizes facilities into performance quadrants based on their Treatment Adherence Rate and Patient Stability Metric relative to median thresholds.
* [Facility with Engaged but High-Impairment Population](facility-with-engaged-but-high-impairment-population.md) - Identifies facilities where the patient population is generally engaged and adherent but continues to struggle with high functional impairment.
* [Facility with High Clinical Leverage Potential](facility-with-high-clinical-leverage-potential.md) - Identifies facilities with a highly engaged and adherent patient population that still experiences significant symptom severity, suggesting readiness for potentially more intensive or alternative interventions.
* [Facility with Potential Engagement-Outcome Disconnect](facility-with-potential-engagement-outcome-disconnect.md) - Identifies facilities where high therapy engagement scores do not seem to translate into expected functional improvements or recovery progression.
* [Facility with Potential Treatment Inertia](facility-with-potential-treatment-inertia.md) - Identifies facilities where patients seem engaged in therapy (high TES) but struggle with overall treatment adherence (low TAR), suggesting potential systemic barriers or resistance.
* [Frequent Crisis Patient](frequent-crisis-patient.md) - Identifies patients with frequent crisis interventions.
* [High Appointment Adherence](high-appointment-adherence.md) - Identifies patients with low missed appointment rates.
* [High-Need, Under-Resourced Facility](high-need-under-resourced-facility.md) - Identifies facilities facing significant aggregate patient risk without adequate community resources.
* [High-Risk Patient](high-risk-patient.md) - Identifies patients with elevated suicide risk or severe symptoms.
* [High Severity, High Risk Patient Group](high-severity-high-risk-patient-group.md) - Identifies patients presenting with both high symptom severity (depression/anxiety) and elevated suicide risk.
* [High Social Support Patient](high-social-support-patient.md) - Identifies patients with strong social support and good relationship quality.
* [Low Engagement Risk](low-engagement-risk.md) - Identifies patients at risk of disengagement from therapy.
* [Non-Compliant Patient](non-compliant-patient.md) - Identifies patients with consistent non-compliance in treatment.
* [Patient Exhibiting Fragile Stability](patient-exhibiting-fragile-stability.md) - Identifies patients currently classified as 'Stable Recovery' but who exhibit risk factors like frequent missed appointments or low social support, suggesting potential for destabilization.
* [Patient with High Crisis & Low Support Profile](patient-with-high-crisis-low-support-profile.md) - Identifies patients characterized by frequent crisis interventions and weak social support systems.
* [Patient with Severe Comorbid Distress Profile](patient-with-severe-comorbid-distress-profile.md) - Identifies patients experiencing significant simultaneous distress across depression, anxiety, and functional domains.
* [Patient with Strong Recovery Capital](patient-with-strong-recovery-capital.md) - Identifies patients demonstrating high social support effectiveness coupled with low functional impairment.
* [Resource-Intensive High-Risk Patient Cohort](resource-intensive-high-risk-patient-cohort.md) - Identifies patients requiring significant care coordination and intervention due to possessing characteristics of both Complex Care Needs and Frequent Crisis Patterns.
* [Resource-Supported Facility](resource-supported-facility.md) - Identifies facilities with adequate or comprehensive community resources.
* [Stable Recovery Patient](stable-recovery-patient.md) - Identifies patients showing stable recovery with good functional outcomes.
* [Stale Treatment Outcome Records](stale-treatment-outcome-records.md) - Treatment outcome records associated with encounters that occurred before a specific time threshold (e.g., older than 60 days).
* [Systemically Stressed Facility Environment](systemically-stressed-facility-environment.md) - Identifies facilities potentially facing overwhelming systemic stress, characterized by a significant gap between patient needs and resources, compounded by high patient attrition risk.
* [Treatment-Resistant Patient](treatment-resistant-patient.md) - Identifies patients with poor treatment response despite adherence.
* [Well-Resourced High-Support Environment](well-resourced-high-support-environment.md) - Identifies facilities that are well-resourced and serve a patient population with generally high levels of social support effectiveness.

# Calculation

* [Adherence Effectiveness Ratio (AER)](adherence-effectiveness-ratio.md) - Calculates a ratio comparing treatment adherence rate to the average functional impairment, suggesting potential treatment impact relative to need.
* [Average GAD-7 Score by Facility (AGSF)](average-gad-7-score-by-facility.md) - Calculates the average GAD-7 anxiety score for patients assessed at a specific facility.
* [Average PHQ-9 Score by Facility (APSF)](average-phq-9-score-by-facility.md) - Calculates the average PHQ-9 depression score for patients assessed at a specific facility.
* [Clinical Improvement Potential Index (CIPI)](clinical-improvement-potential-index.md) - Calculates a ratio comparing patient engagement/adherence to overall symptom severity at a facility, suggesting potential responsiveness to intervention.
* [Comprehensive Facility Risk Score (CFRS)](comprehensive-facility-risk-score.md) - A normalized index assessing overall facility risk based on combined depression severity, suicide risk prevalence, and functional impairment.
* [Correlation Between Resource Adequacy and Adherence (CRAA)](correlation-between-resource-adequacy-and-adherence.md) - Measures the correlation between individual facility resource adequacy scores and treatment adherence rates.
* [Crisis Adherence Ratio (CAR)](crisis-adherence-ratio.md) - Calculates the ratio of crisis intervention frequency to the treatment adherence rate, indicating crises occurring per unit of adherence.
* [Crisis Intervention Frequency (CIF)](crisis-intervention-frequency.md) - Measures the average number of crisis interventions per patient at a facility.
* [Engagement-Adherence Score (EAS)](engagement-adherence-score.md) - Computes a composite score reflecting patient participation and adherence to treatment plans at a facility.
* [Engagement Deficit Index (EDI)](engagement-deficit-index.md) - Quantifies the degree of patient disengagement, considering both therapy engagement scores and appointment attendance.
* [Facility Efficiency Index (FEI)](facility-efficiency-index.md) - Estimates facility efficiency by relating the achieved patient stability metric to the available facility resource adequacy.
* [Facility Resource Adequacy Index (FRAI)](facility-resource-adequacy-index.md) - Quantifies the adequacy of community resources available at a facility.
* [Facility Risk Profile Index (FRPI)](facility-risk-profile-index.md) - Generates an index indicating the overall risk level associated with the patient population at a facility.
* [Missed Appointment Rate (MAR)](missed-appointment-rate.md) - Calculates the average number of missed appointments per patient at a facility.
* [Patient Functional Impairment Score (PFIS)](patient-functional-impairment-score.md) - Calculates an average functional impairment score across patients.
* [Patient Stability Metric (PSM)](patient-stability-metric.md) - Calculates an index reflecting patient stability, inversely related to crisis frequency and missed appointments.
* [Recovery Trajectory Index (RTI)](recovery-trajectory-index.md) - Estimates the effectiveness of treatment adherence in achieving functional improvement at a facility.
* [Resource-Demand Differential (RDD)](resource-demand-differential.md) - Measures the potential gap between average patient functional needs and available facility resources.
* [Social Support Effectiveness (SSE)](social-support-effectiveness.md) - Evaluates the effectiveness of social support based on social support level and relationship quality.
* [Socio-Environmental Support Index (SESI)](socio-environmental-support-index.md) - Computes a composite index reflecting the quality of the patient's social environment and the facility's resource context.
* [Suicide Risk Prevalence (SRP)](suicide-risk-prevalence.md) - Calculates the percentage of assessments indicating high or severe suicide risk at a facility.
* [Support System Pressure Index (SSPI)](support-system-pressure-index.md) - Index assessing the pressure on support systems based on crisis frequency relative to social support effectiveness.
* [Symptom Severity Index (SSI)](symptom-severity-index.md) - Calculates a combined average symptom severity score for a facility, based on depression and anxiety.
* [Therapeutic Alliance & Engagement Score (TAES)](therapeutic-alliance-engagement-score.md) - Computes a combined score reflecting both the average clinician-reported therapeutic alliance and the calculated therapy engagement score for a facility.
* [Therapy Engagement Score (TES)](therapy-engagement-score.md) - Computes an average engagement score across therapy sessions.
* [Treatment Adherence Rate (TAR)](treatment-adherence-rate.md) - Measures the proportion of patients with high or medium treatment adherence at a facility.
