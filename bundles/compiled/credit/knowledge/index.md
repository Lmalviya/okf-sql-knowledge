# Calculation

* [Account Health Index (AHI)](account-health-index.md) - Composite measure of account quality considering age, mix, and payment history.
* [Asset Liquidity Ratio (ALR)](asset-liquidity-ratio.md) - Measures the proportion of customer assets that can be quickly converted to cash.
* [Banking Relationship Strength (BRS)](banking-relationship-strength.md) - Quantifies the depth and quality of a customer's banking relationship.
* [Credit Health Momentum (CHM)](credit-health-momentum.md) - Measures the trajectory of a customer's credit health over time.
* [Credit Health Score (CHS)](credit-health-score.md) - Composite score measuring overall credit wellness based on multiple factors.
* [Credit Quality Index (CQI)](credit-quality-index.md) - Comprehensive measure of overall credit quality incorporating credit score and utilization.
* [Credit Risk Intensity (CRI)](credit-risk-intensity.md) - Advanced measure of credit risk that incorporates payment history and account diversity.
* [Credit Utilization Ratio (CUR)](credit-utilization-ratio.md) - Measures how much of available credit a customer is currently using.
* [Customer Engagement Score (CES)](customer-engagement-score.md) - Quantifies how actively a customer uses bank products and services.
* [Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)](customer-lifetime-value.md) - Measures the total worth of a customer to the financial institution over the entire relationship.
* [Customer Retention Risk (CRR)](customer-retention-risk.md) - Calculates risk of customer attrition based on engagement and satisfaction metrics.
* [Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)](debt-to-income-ratio.md) - Calculates the proportion of a customer's monthly income that goes toward debt payments.
* [Financial Stability Index (FSI)](financial-stability-index.md) - Measures a customer's overall financial stability combining income, savings, and debt factors.
* [Financial Vulnerability Score (FVS)](financial-vulnerability-score.md) - Quantifies financial fragility by combining debt burden and savings adequacy.
* [Housing Affordability Ratio (HAR)](housing-affordability-ratio.md) - Measures the affordability of housing costs relative to income.
* [Investment Portfolio Quality (IPQ)](investment-portfolio-quality.md) - Evaluates the quality and performance of customer's investment allocations.
* [Loan-to-Value Ratio (LTV)](loan-to-value-ratio.md) - Calculates the ratio of loan amount to the value of the asset securing the loan.
* [Net Worth](net-worth.md) - Calculates the financial value of a customer by subtracting liabilities from assets.
* [Risk-Adjusted Return (RAR)](risk-adjusted-return.md) - Measures the profitability of a customer relationship adjusted for credit risk.
* [Total Debt Service Ratio (TDSR)](total-debt-service-ratio.md) - Extended debt ratio that accounts for all financial obligations including housing costs.
