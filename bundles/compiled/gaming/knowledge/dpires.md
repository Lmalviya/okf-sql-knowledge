# Definition

Measured in dots per inch, representing sensor tracking precision. Values around 800-1600 are typically used by professional FPS players for precision, while 12000+ allows for extremely fast cursor movement with minimal physical motion, beneficial for MOBAs and strategy games that require covering large screen areas quickly.
