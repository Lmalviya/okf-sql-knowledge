# Calculation

* [Audio Quality Index (AQI)](audio-quality-index.md) - A comprehensive metric for evaluating audio device quality based on frequency response, distortion, and sensitivity.
* [Battery Efficiency Ratio (BER)](battery-efficiency-ratio.md) - Measures how efficiently a device uses its battery capacity relative to its power draw under active conditions.
* [Comfort Index (CI)](comfort-index.md) - Evaluates the ergonomic comfort of a device based on its physical design factors and ergonomic rating.
* [Competitive Gaming Performance Index (CGPI)](competitive-gaming-performance-index.md) - A comprehensive metric evaluating a device's suitability for competitive gaming based on response time, accuracy, and durability.
* [Durability Score (DS)](durability-score.md) - Quantifies overall device durability based on material quality, environmental resistance, and impact protection.
* [Ergonomic Sustainability Factor (ESF)](ergonomic-sustainability-factor.md) - Evaluates how suitable a device is for extended gaming sessions based on ergonomic design and comfort metrics.
* [Gaming Device Value Index (GDVI)](gaming-device-value-index.md) - A comprehensive metric evaluating overall gaming device value considering performance, durability, and comfort.
* [Gaming Versatility Score (GVS)](gaming-versatility-score.md) - Assesses a device's versatility across different gaming genres and use cases based on adaptability and feature set.
* [Global Efficiency Percentile (GEP)](global-efficiency-percentile.md) - Ranks a device’s Battery Efficiency Ratio (BER) relative to all other wireless gaming devices.
* [Haptic Feedback Quality (HFQ)](haptic-feedback-quality.md) - Measures the quality and effectiveness of haptic feedback systems in gaming controllers and devices.
* [Immersion Enhancement Coefficient (IEC)](immersion-enhancement-coefficient.md) - Quantifies how well a device contributes to gaming immersion through audio quality, haptic feedback, and visual elements.
* [Input Responsiveness Score (IRS)](input-responsiveness-score.md) - Quantifies the overall input responsiveness of a device considering polling rate, latency, and response time.
* [Physical Endurance Rating (PER)](physical-endurance-rating.md) - Measures the physical endurance of a device under intense gaming conditions, combining durability with effective heat management. 
* [Professional Adoption Rating (PAR)](professional-adoption-rating.md) - Quantifies the level of professional gamer adoption and tournament presence of a device based on performance metrics and pro-level features.
* [Response Accuracy Index (RAI)](response-accuracy-index.md) - Measures the accuracy and consistency of input response in gaming devices, accounting for both sensor precision and control stability.
* [RGB Implementation Quality (RIQ)](rgb-implementation-quality.md) - Evaluates the quality of RGB implementation based on brightness, color accuracy, and lighting zones.
* [Sensor Performance Index (SPI)](sensor-performance-index.md) - A composite metric that evaluates overall sensor quality based on resolution, accuracy, and response time.
* [Switch Performance Rating (SPR)](switch-performance-rating.md) - Rates mechanical switch performance based on durability, consistency, and tactile feedback.
* [Value Proposition Index (VPI)](value-proposition-index.md) - A comprehensive metric evaluating a gaming device's overall value by balancing performance, durability, ergonomics, and professional adoption.
* [Wireless Performance Efficiency (WPE)](wireless-performance-efficiency.md) - Evaluates the efficiency of wireless performance relative to battery consumption for untethered gaming devices.
* [Wireless Performance Rating (WPR)](wireless-performance-rating.md) - Rates the quality of wireless connectivity based on range, latency, and interference handling.
