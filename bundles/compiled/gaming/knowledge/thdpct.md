# Definition

Measured as a percentage, representing audio signal distortion at high volumes. Values below 0.1% indicate audiophile-grade reproduction with imperceptible distortion, 0.1-0.5% represents excellent gaming audio quality, 0.5-1.0% is acceptable for most gaming scenarios, while values above 1.0% may produce noticeable distortion during intense gaming sequences.
