# Definition

Measured in actuation cycles before failure. Values around 20 million indicate consumer-grade mechanical switches, 50 million represent premium mechanical switches for enthusiasts, and 100+ million typically denote optical or magnetic switches designed for extended competitive use with minimal degradation over time.
