# Definition

Measured in Hertz (times per second), representing how often the device reports its state to the computer. Values of 125Hz are standard for office peripherals, 500Hz is good for casual gaming, 1000Hz is preferred for competitive play, and 4000Hz+ represents cutting-edge technology offering ultra-responsive input for professional esports.
