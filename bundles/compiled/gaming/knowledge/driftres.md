# Definition

Rated on a scale of 1-10, where values below 5 indicate susceptibility to developing drift over time, 6-8 represents good resistance to drift under normal use conditions, and 9-10 indicates premium Hall Effect or magnetic sensing technologies specifically designed to eliminate drift entirely throughout the controller's lifespan.
