# Definition

Rated on a scale of 1-10, where 1-3 indicates basic designs prioritizing aesthetics over comfort, 4-6 represents good ergonomics for average gaming sessions, 7-8 denotes devices designed for extended comfort, and 9-10 indicates specialized ergonomic designs preventing repetitive strain injuries during marathon sessions.
