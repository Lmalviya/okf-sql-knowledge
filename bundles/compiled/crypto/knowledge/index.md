# Calculation

* [Arbitrage Opportunity Score (AOS)](arbitrage-opportunity-score.md) - Quantifies the potential arbitrage opportunity considering multiple factors.
* [Arbitrage ROI](arbitrage-roi.md) - Calculates the potential return on investment for an arbitrage opportunity.
* [Effective Leverage](effective-leverage.md) - Calculates the actual leverage considering both explicit leverage setting and position size relative to account balance.
* [Liquidity Ratio](liquidity-ratio.md) - Measures the ratio of available liquidity to total market volume.
* [Margin Utilization](margin-utilization.md) - Calculates the percentage of margin being utilized.
* [Market Depth Ratio](market-depth-ratio.md) - Measures the ratio of order book depth to position size to assess market liquidity for position exit.
* [Market Efficiency Ratio (MER)](market-efficiency-ratio.md) - Measures how efficiently orders are executed compared to expected slippage.
* [Market Impact Cost (MIC)](market-impact-cost.md) - Estimates the market impact cost of executing a large order.
* [Order Book Imbalance Ratio](order-book-imbalance-ratio.md) - Quantifies the imbalance between bid and ask sides of the order book.
* [Order Fill Rate](order-fill-rate.md) - Calculates the percentage of an order that has been filled.
* [Position Value at Risk (PVaR)](position-value-at-risk.md) - Calculates the value at risk for a position based on current market conditions.
* [Profit Factor](profit-factor.md) - Measures the ratio of profitable trades to losing trades adjusted for their values.
* [Realized Risk Ratio (RRR)](realized-risk-ratio.md) - Calculates the ratio of realized PnL to position value at risk.
* [Risk-Adjusted Return](risk-adjusted-return.md) - Calculates return on position adjusted for risk exposure.
* [Risk-to-Reward Ratio](risk-to-reward-ratio.md) - Calculates the ratio of potential risk to potential reward for a position.
* [Slippage Impact](slippage-impact.md) - Calculates the expected price slippage impact for a given order size.
* [Smart Money Accuracy](smart-money-accuracy.md) - Measures the success rate of smart money flow in predicting the 4-hour price movement direction.
* [Spread Percentage](spread-percentage.md) - Calculates the spread as a percentage of the midpoint price.
* [Technical Signal Strength](technical-signal-strength.md) - Quantifies the strength of technical signals based on multiple indicators.
* [True Cost of Execution](true-cost-of-execution.md) - Calculates the total cost of order execution including fees and slippage.
* [Volatility-Adjusted Spread](volatility-adjusted-spread.md) - Normalizes the spread by the market volatility to determine if spread is wide relative to expected price movement.
