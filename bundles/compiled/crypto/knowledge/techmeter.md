# Definition

An enum with values 'Buy', 'Sell', or 'Hold'. 'Buy' indicates technical indicators suggest purchasing the asset. 'Sell' indicates technical indicators suggest selling the asset. 'Hold' indicates technical indicators suggest maintaining current positions without new trades.
