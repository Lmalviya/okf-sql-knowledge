# Definition

Fan status typically falls into four categories: 'Active' indicates normal users with full access to all features; 'VIP' represents premium users with privileged services; 'Inactive' refers to dormant accounts with no login for over 30 days, with some features restricted; 'Blocked' means accounts temporarily or permanently banned due to violations, with no access to platform features.
