# Calculation

* [Churn Prevention Investment (CPI)](churn-prevention-investment.md) - Calculates the optimal investment for preventing churn based on fan value and risk
* [Community Contribution Index (CCI)](community-contribution-index.md) - Measures a fan's overall contribution to the virtual idol community
* [Content Creation Impact Score (CCIS)](content-creation-impact-score.md) - Measures the impact of fan-created content on the community
* [Content Quality Consistency (CQC)](content-quality-consistency.md) - Measures the consistency of a fan's content quality relative to their engagement consistency
* [Content Quality to Engagement Ratio (CQER)](content-quality-to-engagement-ratio.md) - Measures how a fan's content quality relates to their overall engagement
* [Event Response Factor (ERF)](event-response-factor.md) - Measures a fan's response rate to official events and activities
* [Event ROI Potential (ERP)](event-roi-potential.md) - Estimates the potential return on investment for inviting a fan to exclusive events
* [Fan Engagement Index (FEI)](fan-engagement-index.md) - A comprehensive metric measuring fan activity level with platform and virtual idols
* [Fan Lifetime Value (FLV)](fan-lifetime-value.md) - Projects the total economic value of a fan throughout their relationship with the platform
* [Gift Impact Quotient (GIQ)](gift-impact-quotient.md) - Evaluates the relative impact of a fan's gifting behavior considering both volume and value
* [Investment Recovery Period (IRP)](investment-recovery-period.md) - Estimates how many months it will take to recover platform investment in a fan
* [Loyalty Progression Rate (LPR)](loyalty-progression-rate.md) - Measures how quickly a fan is accumulating loyalty within the system
* [Loyalty Value Ratio (LVR)](loyalty-value-ratio.md) - Evaluates the relationship between a fan's loyalty points and their economic value
* [Monetization Value (MV)](monetization-value.md) - Calculates the monetary value a fan brings to the platform over time
* [Premium Engagement Ratio (PER)](premium-engagement-ratio.md) - Measures the relationship between fan spending and their engagement level
* [Retention Risk Factor (RRF)](retention-risk-factor.md) - Quantifies the risk of fan churn based on multiple behavioral indicators
* [Social Conversion Rate (SCR)](social-conversion-rate.md) - Measures a fan's ability to convert their social network into active platform participants
* [Social Influence Multiplier (SIM)](social-influence-multiplier.md) - Calculates how much a fan amplifies content through their social network
* [Support Efficiency Index (SEI)](support-efficiency-index.md) - Measures the efficiency of platform resources spent on supporting a fan
* [Tier Acceleration Factor (TAF)](tier-acceleration-factor.md) - Measures how quickly a fan is advancing through tier levels relative to platform norms
