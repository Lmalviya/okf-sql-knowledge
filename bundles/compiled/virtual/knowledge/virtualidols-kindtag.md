# Definition

'2D' represents two-dimensional animated characters, suitable for anime-style performances with limited interactivity; '3D' refers to three-dimensional modeled characters with more natural movements capable of complex performances; 'AI Generated' indicates characters created using AI technology with high uniqueness but requiring consistency monitoring; 'Mixed Reality' describes characters that can interact with real-world elements, requiring the highest technical threshold.
