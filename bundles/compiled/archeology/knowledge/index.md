# Calculation

* [Archaeological Documentation Completeness (ADC)](archaeological-documentation-completeness.md) - Comprehensive score for how completely a site has been documented through scanning with weighted importance factors.
* [Conservation Priority Index (CPI)](conservation-priority-index.md) - Quantifies the urgency of conservation efforts based on site condition, historical significance and structural stability.
* [Digital Preservation Quality (DPQ)](digital-preservation-quality.md) - Comprehensive metric for evaluating digital preservation quality for archaeological sites with weighted quality factors.
* [Environmental Impact Factor (EIF)](environmental-impact-factor.md) - Quantifies how environmental conditions affected scan quality using statistical correlation analysis.
* [Environmental Suitability Index (ESI)](environmental-suitability-index.md) - Evaluates how suitable environmental conditions were for scanning operations using weighted parameters.
* [Equipment Effectiveness Ratio (EER)](equipment-effectiveness-ratio.md) - Evaluates how effectively equipment was utilized based on power consumption and scan quality relative to equipment capability.
* [Feature Extraction Efficiency (FEE)](feature-extraction-efficiency.md) - Measures the efficiency of feature identification in scan data relative to point cloud density and complexity.
* [Mesh Complexity Ratio (MCR)](mesh-complexity-ratio.md) - Measures the topological complexity of a mesh relative to its resolution, helping identify overly complex or simplified archaeological models.
* [Mesh-to-Point Ratio (MPR)](mesh-to-point-ratio.md) - Evaluates the efficiency of mesh generation from point cloud data for optimal decimation determination.
* [Model Fidelity Score (MFS)](model-fidelity-score.md) - Combines mesh complexity, texture quality, and geometric accuracy to assess overall 3D model fidelity for archaeological analysis.
* [Point Cloud Density Ratio (PCDR)](point-cloud-density-ratio.md) - Evaluates the relationship between total points and cloud density, used to assess scan efficiency and data distribution.
* [Processing Efficiency Ratio (PER)](processing-efficiency-ratio.md) - Measures the efficiency of scan processing by comparing processing time to data complexity and size.
* [Processing Resource Utilization (PRU)](processing-resource-utilization.md) - Measures the efficiency of computing resource utilization during scan processing relative to data complexity.
* [Registration Accuracy Ratio (RAR)](registration-accuracy-ratio.md) - Evaluates registration accuracy relative to scan resolution using propagation of uncertainty principles.
* [Scan Coverage Effectiveness (SCE)](scan-coverage-effectiveness.md) - Measures how effectively a scan covers its target area considering both coverage percentage and overlap redundancy.
* [Scan Quality Score (SQS)](scan-quality-score.md) - Comprehensive quality metric combining resolution, coverage, and noise factors with weighted importance.
* [Scan Resolution Index (SRI)](scan-resolution-index.md) - A sophisticated compound index measuring the overall resolution quality of a scan based on resolution and point density.
* [Scan Time Efficiency (STE)](scan-time-efficiency.md) - Measures how efficiently scanning time was used relative to data quality and completeness metrics.
* [Spatial Density Index (SDI)](spatial-density-index.md) - Assesses point cloud density relative to site dimensions for spatial sampling adequacy.
* [Texture Density Index (TDI)](texture-density-index.md) - Evaluates the pixel density of textures relative to mesh resolution for assessing surface detail preservation.
