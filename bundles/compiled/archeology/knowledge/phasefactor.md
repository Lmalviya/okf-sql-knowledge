# Definition

Classifies archaeological sites into standardized chronological/cultural periods. Values like 'Neolithic' (10,000-4,500 BCE), 'Bronze Age' (3,300-1,200 BCE), 'Roman' (27 BCE-476 CE), or 'Medieval' (476-1453 CE) determine applicable research methodologies, conservation approaches, and contextual interpretation frameworks.
