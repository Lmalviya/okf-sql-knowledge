# Definition

Market classes define the primary business model: 'Forum' platforms focus on information exchange and community interactions with minimal direct transactions; 'Service' markets specialize in offering digital services rather than goods; 'Marketplace' denotes traditional product-focused platforms with diverse inventory; 'Exchange' indicates financial transaction services predominantly featuring cryptocurrency trading.
