# Definition

'Consistent' indicates uniform linguistic patterns suggesting single-user accounts with established communication habits; 'Variable' indicates significant linguistic variations that may signal multiple users sharing an account or automated translation tools; 'Suspicious' denotes deliberate attempts to mask writing style through syntax switching or unnatural language patterns often associated with deception attempts.
