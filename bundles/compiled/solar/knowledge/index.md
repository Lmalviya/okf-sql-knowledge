# Calculation

* [Effective Performance Index (EPI)](effective-performance-index.md) - Comprehensive metric that accounts for all factors affecting panel performance.
* [Electrical Degradation Index (EDI)](electrical-degradation-index.md) - Measures the combined degradation of electrical parameters.
* [Energy Production Efficiency (EPE)](energy-production-efficiency.md) - Measures the overall energy production efficiency considering all losses.
* [Expected Energy Yield (EEY)](expected-energy-yield.md) - Calculates the expected energy production considering current conditions.
* [Fill Factor Degradation Rate (FFDR)](fill-factor-degradation-rate.md) - Calculates the rate at which the fill factor of a panel is degrading over time.
* [Financial Impact of Degradation (FID)](financial-impact-of-degradation.md) - Calculates the financial cost of degradation over time.
* [Grid Integration Quality (GIQ)](grid-integration-quality.md) - Measures the overall quality of power delivered to the grid.
* [Inverter Efficiency Loss (IEL)](inverter-efficiency-loss.md) - Calculates the energy lost due to inverter inefficiency.
* [Irradiance Utilization Ratio (IUR)](irradiance-utilization-ratio.md) - Measures how effectively the panel converts available solar irradiance to power.
* [Maintenance Cost Efficiency (MCE)](maintenance-cost-efficiency.md) - Evaluates the cost-effectiveness of maintenance relative to the plant's capacity.
* [Maintenance Return on Investment (MROI)](maintenance-return-on-investment.md) - Evaluates the financial return of maintenance activities.
* [Normalized Degradation Index (NDI)](normalized-degradation-index.md) - Compares panel degradation to expected rates based on panel type and age.
* [Panel Efficiency Loss Rate (PELR)](panel-efficiency-loss-rate.md) - Calculates the percentage of efficiency loss per year for a solar panel.
* [Panel Performance Ratio (PPR)](panel-performance-ratio.md) - Measures how well a solar panel is performing relative to its rated power.
* [Revenue Loss Rate (RLR)](revenue-loss-rate.md) - Calculates the revenue loss per MW of capacity due to maintenance issues.
* [Soiling Impact Factor (SIF)](soiling-impact-factor.md) - Quantifies the impact of soiling on power production based on dust density.
* [Temperature Adjusted Performance Ratio (TAPR)](temperature-adjusted-performance-ratio.md) - Enhances the Panel Performance Ratio by accounting for temperature effects.
* [Temperature Performance Coefficient Impact (TPCI)](temperature-performance-coefficient-impact.md) - Quantifies the impact of temperature on panel performance based on its temperature coefficient.
* [Total System Loss (TSL)](total-system-loss.md) - Calculates the combined power losses from all major sources.
* [Weather Corrected Efficiency (WCE)](weather-corrected-efficiency.md) - Adjusts panel efficiency measurements to account for weather conditions.
